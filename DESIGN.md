# Design — SOLARIS

A bright architecture practice built around sunlight as a material. Huge Manrope typography and unframed architectural scenes, with butter #f6edb6, paper #faf8ee, ink #171b1e and ultramarine #213ed4. The sunburst resolves beside three staggered headline lines, then the photographic courtyard opens beneath. Native scroll moves into a large statement, offset selected-work images, a real 3D light laboratory and a blue invitation.

This is deliberately distinct from STILLFORM's beige framed photo journal and FOLDFIELD's paper mechanics. Scale is inhabited architecture, imagery is contemporary and luminous, and the remembered interaction is moving sunlight through the open pavilion skylight.

Home → selected studies → detail → next study → studio → local enquiry is complete. The project index filters Residential, Cultural and Interiors. Hash routes preserve browser history; navigation closes on routing and main receives focus. No invented team biographies, awards, clients or commercial results.

Phone layouts recompose type/sun/photo rather than shrinking the desktop. The courtyard image receives a tall crop; the 3D study has its own full-width viewport. Camera switches are button controls; touch scrolling is never captured by a canvas.

GSAP scoped useGSAP owns headline arrival and section reveals, ScrollTrigger owns mild image drift. Reduced motion renders final states. React owns route and control state; Three owns scene and camera. The pavilion draws only dirty frames, pauses offscreen/hidden and disposes resources.
